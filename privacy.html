How Claudionoid collects, uses and protects your data.

DataWhy

3. Legal basis (GDPR)

  • Contract: providing the service you signed up for.
  • Legitimate interest: security, abuse prevention, service improvement.
  • Consent: for photos analysed by Looksmax and any health information you choose to share. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation: billing and tax records.

4. Sensitive information

The Health, Spiritual and Looksmax humanoids may receive information about your health, beliefs or appearance. Only share what you are comfortable with. This data is processed only to answer your request and is not used for advertising or profiling.

We do not sell your data. Some providers are located outside the EU; transfers are covered by Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. Retention

Account and usage data are kept while your account is active and deleted within 30 days of account deletion. Billing records may be kept by Stripe for up to 7 years as required by law.

7. Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data, object to processing, or withdraw consent. Email support@claudionoid.com or delete your account. You may also complain to your local data protection authority (in Portugal: CNPD).

8. Children

Claudionoid is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Passwords are hashed. Access to production systems is restricted.

10. Changes

We will notify you in the app or by email before material changes take effect.
