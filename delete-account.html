Delete your account

You can delete your Claudionoid account and all associated data at any time.

Fastest: inside the app

Open Claudionoid → Settings → Account → Delete account and confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

No access to the app?

  1. Email support@claudionoid.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request".
  2. Send it from the email address linked to your account.
  3. We confirm your identity and delete the account within 30 days.
  4. You receive a confirmation email when it is done.
Send deletion request →
Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Cancel any active subscription first in Settings → Plan to avoid further charges.
